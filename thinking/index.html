Thinking

The evidence, and what to do with it.

Writing on where climate, health and access meet. Less about what is coming, more about what is already happening inside operations, supply chains and the communities organisations depend on.

The route every programme here is built along.

Three points, in the order they happen. Everything in Thinking, the guide and the calculator is about closing the distance between the second and the third. It is where SDG 3, good health, and SDG 13, climate action, stop being separate goals.

1

A climate event moves a family.

A flood, a heat season, a cyclone. People leave, for a week or for good. This part is on the record and it is getting more frequent.

2

Their medicine and their entitlements stay behind.

The prescription is at a pharmacy they no longer pass. The scheme they qualify for is registered to an address they left. A person on chronic medication runs out first.

3

The programme is built to follow them.

Continuity designed in before the event, not reconstructed after it. Measured as a cohort, so the number holds in front of a board or an assurer. That is the work.
