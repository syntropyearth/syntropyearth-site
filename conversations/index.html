What stops CSR money turning into outcomes people can see?

Forty minutes with the people who run these budgets, the people close to the ground, and the researchers who sharpen the argument. Asked what is hard in their role, not pitched anything. Each conversation lands here as a page: what was said, what surprised us, what it changes about how programmes get built.

Who gets invited

People who own or control a budget: owner-operator CEOs and MDs, CSR heads, foundation leads. People close to the ground: district health officers, NGO founders, programme leads. Researchers who test the thesis. No paid relationships on any side.

Say yes to forty minutes

If your seat sits on top of the question, the invitation is open. If you are comfortable, it goes out on YouTube and LinkedIn. If not, it stays between us and still shapes what gets built.
