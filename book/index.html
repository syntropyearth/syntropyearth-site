Book a site call

Twenty minutes. One site. One summer.

Bring a site or a school in mind. We ask about the place, the work and last summer. You leave knowing what would be measured there, what you would receive at the end, and what it would ask of your team. No deck, no pitch.

Before the call

Three things worth having to hand.

  • Roughly how many people are on the site or in the school, and how many work or play outdoors
  • What happened last summer that you would rather not repeat
  • Who would own a heat protocol on your side: a safety officer, a facility head, a principal
