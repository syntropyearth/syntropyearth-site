What we do

We put instruments where your people work or learn, measure one full hot season, and hand you the proof and the plan.

01

Measure

Heat stress, read as wet-bulb globe temperature every 15 minutes at the point of work and the point of rest.

02

Act

A four-band card on the wall. Your own supervisors or teachers run it, twice a day.

03

Prove

A report three weeks after the season, mapped to BRSR, GRI 403 and two SDG targets.

What you hold at the end

The season ends. The evidence stays with you.

01

The Site Heat Report

Exposure by hour, days in each band, actions, cases, and three changes for next year.

02

The reporting paragraph

In the disclosure's own voice, with the numbers the season counted.

Outcomes, counted
