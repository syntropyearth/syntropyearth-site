Evidence and the register · heat-health data from the site

What a measured season leaves behind.

A season produces three things that outlast it: an evidence pack in your hands, a record in the register, and a number that can be compared with next year. This page shows each one, and says plainly what exists today and what is still being built.

Work in progressThe first measured heat season runs March to July 2027. Until then, every chart on this page is an illustrative preview with invented values, and is labelled so. Measured, anonymised records replace them from August 2027.
The evidence pack

What you receive, and where each piece reports.

One pack per site, three weeks after the season. Each line below is written to be placed straight into a document you already produce.

  1. Site Heat Report or School Heat Report

    Exposure, days in each band, actions, cases, outcomes against baseline, three recommendations.

    Goes toBoard paper; project file; parents and school board

  2. Heat-safe protocol for the site

    Band card, rest and water rule, alert-day actions, referral path. Signed by the site head or the principal.

    Maps toBRSR Principle 3; GRI 403-1, 403-2; ISO 45001 hazard register; state heat action plan

  3. BRSR paragraph

    Worker well-being and safety, with counted numbers, including contract workers.

    Maps toBRSR Principle 3; Principle 6 where heat is reported as a physical climate risk

  4. GRI 403 table

    Each season number set against the occupational health and safety disclosures.

    Maps toGRI 403-3, 403-6, 403-9, 403-10

  5. SDG lines

    Two primary targets with the indicator the season reports against each.

    Maps toSDG 3.d and 8.8; secondary 13.1; 4.a for schools

  6. CSR tag and project record

    For a funded cohort: the Schedule VII item and a pack written to serve the committee's project assessment.

    Maps toSchedule VII items (i) and (ii)

  7. Beneficiary outcomes

    Cases caught early, referrals completed, days lost, attendance on alert days, people reached by cohort.

    Goes toThe impact number in the annual report

"Maps to" means the pack is written to serve that disclosure or standard. Whether it satisfies it is for your assurer or counsel to confirm. BRSR is SEBI's Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report; GRI 403 is the Global Reporting Initiative standard on occupational health and safety (2018).
The register · interactive preview

Three views. Each one pushes a different person to act.

The register holds every site-season in one shape. The same record reads three ways: for one site, across a district, across the country. Choose a view. Move over the chart.

Preview · illustrative, not measured
Site view

For the person who runs the site

Your own season against the bands. Which weeks cost the most. Whether the protocol held on red days.

District view

For the person with many sites

Sites in one district against each other and against the benchmark. Where to measure next. Which protocol is working.

State view

For funders and public bodies

Where measured site-seasons exist and where there are none. Where a grant or a state programme would add the most.

Who sees what: a site sees its own full record. Funders, researchers and public bodies see anonymised aggregates. Nothing in the register identifies a worker or a pupil.
